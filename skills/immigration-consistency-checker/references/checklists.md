# Default package checklists

These are **starting points, not authority**. The firm's own checklist overrides them, and
the form instructions on uscis.gov are the final word. Required evidence changes, so confirm
against the current form instructions before relying on any line here.

## Family: I-130 (spouse)
- G-28 (if represented), signed by the attorney and client
- I-130, signed by the petitioner; I-130A for the spouse beneficiary
- Proof of petitioner's status (US passport, naturalisation certificate or green card)
- Marriage certificate; proof all prior marriages ended
- Evidence of a bona fide marriage
- Passport-style photos where the instructions require them
- Fee (current schedule) or G-1450
- Translations with certifications

## Adjustment: I-485 package
- I-485 signed; I-864 with tax evidence; I-693 (sealed, where required at filing)
- Birth certificate; passport biographic page; I-94; evidence of lawful entry
- Optional I-765 / I-131
- Fee(s) per the current schedule

## Employment: I-129 (H-1B)
- Certified LCA matching the job title, SOC code, worksite and wage on the I-129
- Degree evidence and evaluations; job description; employer support letter
- Beneficiary passport, I-94 and status history

## EB-5: I-526E
- I-526E signed by the investor
- Evidence of investment (subscription agreement, transfer records)
- Lawful source and path of funds evidence (see eb5-source-of-funds-mapper)
- Regional center / project documents as required
- Investor identity documents; translations
- Fee per the current schedule; note any integrity fund or deadline-specific rules

## Naturalisation: N-400
- N-400 signed; copy of green card front and back
- Travel history matching passport stamps and the I-94 record
- Marital history and evidence where the case relies on it
- Fee or fee reduction request

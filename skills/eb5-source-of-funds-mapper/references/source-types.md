# Common EB-5 source types and typical supporting evidence

A starting checklist only. Confirm current requirements in the USCIS Policy Manual and form
instructions, and follow the attorney's judgement about what a particular case needs.

| Source | Typical evidence |
|---|---|
| Salary / employment | Employment letters, payslips, tax returns, bank deposits matching salary |
| Business ownership / profits | Company registration, ownership records, audited financials, tax filings, dividend records |
| Sale of property | Purchase and sale contracts, title records, proof of how the original purchase was funded, proceeds deposit, taxes paid |
| Sale of shares / assets | Acquisition and sale records, brokerage statements, proceeds trail |
| Gift | Gift letter, donor relationship evidence, **the donor's own lawful source evidence**, transfer records, any gift tax filings |
| Loan | Loan agreement, lender identity, security or collateral evidence (and its lawful source), transfer records |
| Inheritance | Will or probate records, estate documents, transfer records |
| Currency conversion / transfers | Exchange receipts, wire confirmations, records of any intermediaries, evidence of compliance with currency-export rules in the source country |

Flags that often draw RFEs: large unexplained deposits, cash, third-party transfers, gaps in
statements, name mismatches, and circular transfers.

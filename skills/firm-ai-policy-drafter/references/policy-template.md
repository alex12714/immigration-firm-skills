**Effective:** [date]   **Owner:** [name]   **Review:** every 6 months

## 1. Who this covers
Everyone who works on firm matters: attorneys, paralegals, intake, admin, contractors and
outsourced staff.

## 2. Red lines
- No client names, identifiers (A-numbers, passport, SSN, receipt numbers), documents or
  financial records in any personal or unapproved AI account. No exceptions for deadlines.
- No AI output is filed, sent to a client or relied on without human review.

## 4. Allowed / not allowed
Allowed (approved tools): first drafts of letters and summaries, translation drafts,
checklists, consistency checks, research starting points.
Not allowed: final legal advice, unreviewed filings, uploading data for [sensitive case
types] without attorney approval.

## 5. Anonymise first
Outside approved tools, replace identifiers: "Investor A", "[DOB]", "[Employer]".

## 6. Review and responsibility
A qualified person reviews every output. The responsible attorney owns anything filed or
sent to a client.

## 7. Verify everything
Citations, facts, form editions, fees, deadlines and processing times are checked against
the primary source (uscis.gov, the statute, the regulation, the Policy Manual).

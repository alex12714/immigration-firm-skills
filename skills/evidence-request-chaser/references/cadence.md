# Follow-up cadence (default; the firm can override)

| Day | Message | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Full checklist with examples and the upload link | Welcoming, clear |
| 5 | Friendly progress note: received vs outstanding | Light |
| 10 | Reminder with the consequence stated once | Clear, kind |
| 15 | Offer a 10-minute call to solve blockers | Helpful |
| 20 | Flag to the attorney; the attorney or case manager calls | Personal |

Time-sensitive cases (a filing deadline, an RFE response, the EB-5 grandfathering deadline)
compress the schedule: work back from the deadline, leaving the attorney at least 5 business
days to review the complete file.
